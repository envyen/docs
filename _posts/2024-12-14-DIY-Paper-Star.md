Crafting your own paper star is a fun and rewarding project that’s perfect for decorating your home, celebrating the holidays, or simply adding a handmade touch to any event. 
With just a few materials and some simple folding techniques, you can create a stunning star that radiates charm and creativity.

<div class="gallery">
<img src="../../upload/diy-star/diy-star-min.jpg">
</div>

Materials you’ll need:

- White or Colored paper
- Scissors
- Glue stick
- Ruler
- Pencil
- String or thread (optional, for hanging)

Steps:

1. Draw the outlines of a single side as below <img src="../../upload/diy-star/star-one.png">
2. Make 5 similar outlines
3. Puch holes for decoration
4. Cut along the outer edges
5. Fold all the inner lines
6. Glue the outer folding flaps to make a cone
7. Make 5 such cones and glue them together to make a star
8. Note to keep one edge open to tie string or insert a light
